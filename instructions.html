How to use MealDaddy

Start simple. Add detail when you want more personal guidance.

MealDaddy.AI works best when it knows your goals, what you are eating, where you are eating, and which tracking tools you want enabled. Use the quickstart once, then log meals with a photo plus a short description for the deepest interpretation.

Core systems

The engines behind MealDaddy.

Nutrition Tracking Engine

Tracks calories, protein, total carbs, fiber, net carbs, fat, and hydration. Net carbs are calculated as total carbs minus fiber, and remaining daily net carbs are always displayed.

Defaults Engine

Applies neutral assumptions when details are missing, allows user override, avoids hard-coded habits, and flags uncertainty when needed.

Adaptive Hydration

Uses a user-defined daily water goal, tracks fluids, supports pacing reminders, and can adjust hydration guidance after salty meals.

Inflammation Score System

Scores meals from 1 strongly anti-inflammatory to 10 highly inflammatory using processing level, refined carbs, added sugars, seed oils, alcohol, and whole-food ratio.

Reporting System

Creates daily reports, weekly reports, and monthly reports on request, including macros, net carb compliance, hydration, inflammation trends, insights, and action fixes.

Smart Coaching Engine

Triggers suggestions when patterns appear: elevated inflammation scores, high stress trends, low energy, hydration gaps, or nutritional imbalance.

Restaurant Mode

When a restaurant is mentioned, MealDaddy recommends optimal menu choices, identifies high-risk items, suggests substitutions, estimates macros, and applies inflammation guidance.

Feedback and Export

After reports, users can rate the output from 1 to 5, add comments, and request exports such as "Export my week" or "Create my log file."

Commands and examples

Useful phrases to try.

Setup

Units: US. Track BMI: Yes. Weight: [optional]. Goals: calories, protein, net carbs, fiber, water. Optional: steps, sleep, stress.

Log a meal

Photo attached. Description: 3 eggs, mushrooms, herbs, olive oil. Estimate nutrition, net carbs, fiber, hydration impact, and Inflammation Score.

Restaurant mode

I am going to [restaurant]. Recommend the best menu choices for my goals and suggest substitutions.

Finish the day

Finish my day. Include daily summary, net carb compliance, inflammation score, hydration progress, key insights, and tomorrow's plan.

Weight and BMI

Override BMI with [value]. Clear BMI override. Show my weight trend as loss, gain, or stable.

Export

Export my week. Create my log file. Include a daily log sheet and weekly summary sheet.

Database mode

Use verified nutrition data when available. Add new foods from a label or photo and keep the data structured.

Guardrails

Do not guess when reliable data exists. Flag uncertainty clearly. Keep outputs structured, readable, and logically consistent.

BMI and nutrition scoring are general guidance tools, not diagnostic tools. MealDaddy.AI does not replace medical advice from a qualified clinician.
