Photos count each image sent in a successful provider response, including repeated analysis and results that later fail validation. Attachments alone do not count. Restaurant research groups its search and formatting calls into one request; a processed request means at least one provider call returned successfully, not necessarily a usable recommendation.

Per-meal averages use all currently saved meal entries created in the same effective UTC period across the shared account database, including entries without AI. Beta and production costs are shown separately; their denominators share those meals. Deleted entries are excluded. Saved-food and leftover photo analysis are included in photo totals.

Costs are USD estimates from reported tokens and recorded search calls at standard list rates, not provider invoices. Photo cost includes the entire analysis request, not just image tokens. Unknown prices or missing usage remain unpriced, never zero. Per-unit cost is withheld when any calls are unpriced. Personal-key costs are paid by the member; sponsor costs are separate from hosted MealDaddy usage. Storage, taxes, discounts and other services are excluded. Tracking begins at rollout; earlier activity is not reconstructed.
