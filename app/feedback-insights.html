How this analysis protects customer information

Exact counts and rating changes are calculated on Meal Daddy's server. Only an authorized owner can request or view this dashboard.

When a fresh summary is requested, ratings and comments are grouped under temporary customer aliases and processed by OpenAI. Email addresses and account identifiers are not sent, and the request is configured not to be stored by the model provider.

AI themes and their evidence counts are review indicators, not formal survey statistics. Check the exact measurements above before making a decision.
