Plain-language privacy

How Meal Daddy handles information

✓
Account & food records

Stored in your protected Meal Daddy account so they can sync across your devices. Row-level security limits access to your account.

Meal photos

Uploaded privately only when you choose a photo. Processing photos are temporary and removed after the request; deletion also removes any remaining uploads.

AI processing

Only the information needed for a meal estimate or requested guidance is sent to the approved AI service. Provider selection changes which AI service processes that request.

Location

Restaurant Mode asks only when you tap the location control. Approximate location can inform that request and is not saved to your profile or ledger.

Payments

Stripe processes payment methods, subscription status, and invoices. Meal Daddy receives billing status and Stripe reference IDs, not your complete card number.

Health context

Optional nutrition and health preferences help personalize general wellness guidance. Meal Daddy is not medical care and does not diagnose or treat conditions.

Permanent deletion

Delete my account

This is intentionally harder than cancellation because it cannot be undone.

!
  • Your subscription is canceled immediately so it cannot renew.
  • Your profile, setup answers, ledger, nutrition estimates, feedback history, and remaining private photos are permanently deleted.
  • Past invoices and records Stripe must keep for financial or legal purposes are not erased from Stripe.
  • Deleting does not automatically refund a prior charge. Use billing management first if you only want to stop renewal.
