# MealDaddy brand and trademark register

Status: internal working register for counsel review. This document records intended brand use; it is not a legal clearance opinion or proof of registration.

## Use rules

- Use `™` on the first prominent appearance of an unregistered claimed mark on a screen or page. Repeating it on every occurrence is unnecessary and visually distracting.
- Never use `®` unless the exact mark has a current federal registration covering the relevant goods or services.
- Use each mark consistently as a brand name, not as a generic noun or verb.
- Keep dated screenshots, releases, marketing pages, and repository history as evidence of actual use.
- Obtain a comprehensive federal, state, common-law, domain, app-store, and international clearance search before filing or expanding internationally.

## Evidence to preserve

For each release, preserve the release date, commit identifier, representative screenshots, public URL, exact mark spelling, feature description, and the goods or services offered. Record the first verified public-use date only from evidence; do not infer or backdate it.
