Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026. This draft must receive qualified legal review before broad promotion.

Information MealDaddy handles

MealDaddy may handle account details, profile and nutrition goals, food preferences and restrictions, height and weight information you choose to provide, meal descriptions and photos, nutrition estimates, saved foods, feedback, and approximate location when you deliberately request nearby restaurant guidance.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate your account, operate meal logging and reports, personalize guidance, estimate nutrition, provide support, prevent abuse, maintain security, and administer memberships.

Account storage and syncing

MealDaddy stores account-linked information to support access to your records across devices and supported platforms. Profile preferences, meal history, nutrition estimates, saved foods selected for account sync, and progress records can be stored in your protected account. Where offered, device-only and one-time storage choices remain available; device-only records do not sync and can be lost if the device's app or browser data is cleared.

No sale of user data

MealDaddy does not sell user data, including email addresses, meal histories, photos, nutrition preferences, and progress records. Information is processed by service providers to operate MealDaddy as described below.

Service providers and AI processing

Supabase provides authentication, database, and private storage services. Stripe handles payment methods, subscriptions, invoices, and billing management; MealDaddy does not store full card numbers. OpenAI currently processes selected meal text, images, profile context, feedback, or approximate location when an AI feature requires it. Browser speech-recognition features may use services provided by your browser or device vendor.

Photos and location

Ordinary meal photos are intended to be transient unless you deliberately save food evidence or a before-and-after record. Before the web app uploads a supported photo, it re-encodes the image to remove embedded source-file metadata such as EXIF and GPS data. Device-only reference files remain on that device. Location is requested only after you invoke Restaurant Mode, is reduced in precision for the request, and is not intended to be stored in your profile or ledger.

Retention and your choices

Account information is retained while needed to provide the service and meet security, legal, and financial obligations. The Account page provides export, subscription management, and permanent deletion controls. Stripe may retain financial records it is legally required to keep. Service-provider security and abuse-monitoring retention may continue for limited periods after a request or deletion.

Changes in business ownership

If MealDaddy is acquired or its business is transferred to a successor, user information may transfer as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law and the privacy commitments under which it was collected. An ownership change does not by itself authorize unrelated use or sale of previously collected user data.

We will notify users of a change in who controls their data and explain any proposed material changes to its use. Any changed practices must comply with applicable law, with consent obtained where required.

Health information

MealDaddy is a consumer nutrition tool and is not represented as a HIPAA-covered healthcare service. Do not use MealDaddy for emergencies or as a substitute for a qualified clinician.

Security and incidents

MealDaddy uses authenticated accounts, user-scoped database controls, private server functions, and encrypted network transport. No internet service can promise absolute security. MealDaddy will investigate suspected incidents and provide notices when required by applicable law.
