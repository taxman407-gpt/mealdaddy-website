Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026. This draft must receive qualified legal review before broad promotion.

Information MealDaddy handles

MealDaddy may handle account details, profile and nutrition goals, food preferences and restrictions, height and weight information you choose to provide, meal descriptions and photos, nutrition estimates, saved foods, feedback, and approximate location when you deliberately request nearby restaurant guidance.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate your account, operate meal logging and reports, personalize guidance, estimate nutrition, provide support, prevent abuse, maintain security, and administer memberships. MealDaddy does not sell personal information.

Service providers and AI processing

Supabase provides authentication, database, and private storage services. Stripe handles payment methods, subscriptions, invoices, and billing management; MealDaddy does not store full card numbers. OpenAI currently processes selected meal text, images, profile context, feedback, or approximate location when an AI feature requires it. Browser speech-recognition features may use services provided by your browser or device vendor.

Photos and location

Ordinary meal photos are intended to be transient unless you deliberately save food evidence or a before-and-after record. Location is requested only after you invoke Restaurant Mode, is reduced in precision for the request, and is not intended to be stored in your profile or ledger.

Retention and your choices

Account information is retained while needed to provide the service and meet security, legal, and financial obligations. The Account page provides export, subscription management, and permanent deletion controls. Stripe may retain financial records it is legally required to keep. Service-provider security and abuse-monitoring retention may continue for limited periods after a request or deletion.

Health information

MealDaddy is a consumer nutrition tool and is not represented as a HIPAA-covered healthcare service. Do not use MealDaddy for emergencies or as a substitute for a qualified clinician.

Security and incidents

MealDaddy uses authenticated accounts, user-scoped database controls, private server functions, and encrypted network transport. No internet service can promise absolute security. MealDaddy will investigate suspected incidents and provide notices when required by applicable law.
