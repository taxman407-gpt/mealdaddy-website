Health and Nutrition Disclaimer

Last updated: August 12, 2026. This draft must receive qualified legal review before broad promotion.

MealDaddy provides educational estimates and general food-pattern guidance. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, prescriptions, emergency monitoring, or professional medical or dietetic care.

Estimates are not measurements

Calories, nutrients, portions, hydration, BMI, inflammation scores, and projections are estimates. Photos, descriptions, restaurant information, product formulas, preparation methods, and serving sizes may be incomplete or change. Verify nutrition labels and restaurant information when accuracy matters.

Inflammation and BMI

Inflammation scores describe estimated food-pattern characteristics; they are not biomarkers or diagnoses. BMI calculated from self-reported height and weight is a screening estimate and not a professional body-composition measurement.

Personal medical needs

Consult a qualified clinician or registered dietitian about allergies, pregnancy, eating disorders, diabetes, kidney or heart conditions, medication effects, or other medical nutrition needs. Never delay professional care because of MealDaddy output. For an emergency, contact local emergency services.
