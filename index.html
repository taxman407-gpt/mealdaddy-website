Why it feels useful

Customizations that make the program feel alive.

Health goal profiles

Tune plans for weight control, eating healthier, lower carb meals, higher protein, balanced portions, or simple consistency.

Condition-aware preferences

Set preferences such as type 2 diabetes-friendly choices, lower sugar, heart-smart meals, or sodium awareness.

Restaurant finder

Search a geographic area for restaurants that fit your goals, preferences, budget, and eating style before you decide where to go.

Life-aware planning

Layer in pantry items, family preferences, budget, servings, allergies, cooking skill, and the time you really have.

Coaching tone

Pick calm helper, funny coach, straight shooter, or budget-minded guide so the advice lands the way you need it.

Smart swaps

Turn cravings and menu items into practical alternatives: better sides, leaner proteins, lower sugar options, and easier portion choices.

Order customization

Adjust restaurant choices to your profile with sauce, side, portion, carb, protein, and drink recommendations that fit your goals.

Menu translation

Turn confusing menu language into plain guidance, with notes on what helps your goals and what may be worth modifying.

Feedback reports

See meal breakdowns, macros, daily summaries, and end-of-day ledgers that turn choices into useful feedback instead of guilt.

Progress loop

Spot patterns across home meals, restaurant orders, swaps, portions, and goal alignment so tomorrow's plan starts smarter.

Adaptive Hydration

Track water goals that adjust to the food you eat, including salty restaurant meals that may call for a higher target.

Inflammation score

Review meals through an inflammation-aware lens so users can notice patterns around sodium, fiber, fats, carbs, and ingredients.
